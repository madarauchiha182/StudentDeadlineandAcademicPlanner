FEATURE 1 • SMART PRIORITY LEVELS

Multi-Factor Priority Calculation & Badges

🧠 4-Vector Algorithmic Suggestion
  • 1. Proximity to Deadline: Exact diff in hours between simulated date (26 Oct 2026) and due timestamp.
  • 2. Assignment Weightage: High (Exams/Major Projects), Medium (Standard Journals), Low (Prep/Reading).
  • 3. Estimated Effort: Workload hours required compared against available daily study blocks.
  • 4. Completion Percentage: Tasks with low progress (<50%) automatically escalate priority as deadlines near.
🔴 Critical
Due ≤ 24h with <75% done, or High Weightage due in ≤ 2 days.
🟠 High
Due in ≤ 3 days, or remaining effort > 4 study hours.
🟡 Medium
Standard assignments due in 4–7 days with average workload.
🟢 Low
Minor prep or tasks due > 7 days out or already > 80% complete.

0–100% Progress Tracking & Subtasks Breakdown
